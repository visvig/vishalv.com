### ABSTRACT










> psychostimulants, such as R-modafinil, enhance cognitive performance without inducing significant euphoria or addiction, making them promising lead candidates for clinical application
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=U4IP2SBK))










> R-modafinil analogs with extended residence time at DAT (i.e., slow koff) would enhance cognitive function more effectively
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=62JX7XA8))










> evaluated a series of R-modafinil analogs using in vitro equilibrium and non-equilibrium measurements, in vivo fast-scan cyclic voltammetry, and highly translational cognitive assays in both healthy and scopolamine-treated rats modelling cognitive impairment
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=QLBXXVIB))





Scopolamine blocks acetylcholine from binding to muscarinic receptor.

It is a competitive antagonist.

This results in impaired memory and attention.






> prolonging DAT occupancy improves dopamine signalling and leads to more robust enhancements in cognitive flexibility
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=W6KXLL7T))










> S-MK-26 and (S,S)-CE158—produced the strongest cognitive effects
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=UAGLAKEW))










### 1. Introduction










> (DAT) is a key protein in spatially and temporally shaping the dopaminergic signal
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=F65XVY9L))










> DAT reuptakes the dopamine (DA) released by exocytosis from the  extracellular space and moves it back into the presynaptic terminals
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=9SHMLDRE))





Exocytosis: process of releasing molecules outside.

Dopamine molecules are stored inside tiny membrane-bound sacs called vesicles.





> single-point mutations altering DAT function are associated with a variety of psychiatric disorders
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=1&annotation=BAX2NTIH))










> inhibitors if they bind to the transporter and prevent neurotransmitter uptake
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=M56758J5))





Cocaine, Modafinil, Benztropine.





> releasers if they act as substrates and reverse the normal direction of transporter flux
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=S2Q7FWNE))





Methamphetamine.





> inhibitors or releasers, many compounds interacting with DAT stimulate different brain functions and are normally classified as psychostimulants
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=HA7LIEUG))










> Drugs that act more potently with DAT compared to the serotonin transporter (SERT) are typically associated with higher psychostimulant and abuse potential
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=4QJ972K6))










> DAT inhibitors, such as cocaine, despite showing an unselective DAT/SERT profile, still show pronounced psychostimulant effects and high abuse liability
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=8WQX2TLT))










> benztropine possesses high DAT selectivity but lacks pronounced reinforcing properties
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=G4F8NPYX))










> benztropines stabilize the inward-facing conformation of DAT, which differs from the typical binding mode of classical DAT inhibitors such as cocaine
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=KQ7BGL95))





Ti state.






> modafinil or (S)-amphetamine, promote wakefulness without producing marked euphoria
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=ELSVET4L))










> weak interaction with DAT and the existence of related analogs with even lower DAT affinity and more powerful eugeroics properties made questioning the importance of DAT in the cognitive enhancing properties elicited by eugeroics
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=EP7ZA8BR))





Eugeroics: Wakefulness promoting drugs.





> new modafinil analog (S,S)-CE-158 was shown to lack off-target effects
([2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=2&annotation=P2TESM9B))










> promnestic effects

Memory enhancing.





> DAT as the central and clinically relevant target responsible for the cognitive effects

> faster is the DA increase induced by a given psychostimulant, the stronger is the high perceived, and therefore the risk of misuse

### 2.9. Statistics










> Comparisons between two independent groups were performed using two-tailed unpaired t-tests
([4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=4&annotation=Z477FKY4))





t-test: (Mu_A - Mu_B) / (SE) gives the number of std errors away from null (0).

large t-value is a strong evidence against null hypothesis.






> more than two groups, we used a one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey&#39;s post hoc multiple-comparison test
([4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=4&annotation=MBPYJKU5))





ANOVA: Analysis of Variance.

F = MS_between / MS_within

Tukey: Check which specific pairs differ.

q = |X_i - X_j| / SE






> across ASST stages, we applied a two-way mixed-effects ANOVA with  Sˇíd ́ak&#39;s correction or Uncorrected Fisher&#39;s LSD for multiple comparisons
([4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=4&annotation=4VMWLJRR))





two-way-ANOVA:

F_treatment
F_stage
F_interaction

Sidak's: 

alpha_ind = 1 - (1-alpha)^(1/m)

Fisher's LSD:

t = (X_i - X_j)/sqrt(MS_error (1/ni + 1/nj))






> Normality of data distributions was assessed using the Kolmogorov–Smirnov test
([4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=4&annotation=HECWEBV9))





Kolmogorov-Smirnov Test:

D = sup |Fn(x) - F(x)|

### 4. Discussion










> DAT/SERT selectivity ratio, is regularly used as a predictive indicator of the abuse liability of a given drug
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=B4H49ZIE))





But there are exceptions, like cocaine.





> benztropines, rimcazole, GBR12909, and related analogs do not induce robust reinforcing
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=2GTKASDY))











> cocaine, which induces reinforcing and stimulatory effects, does not present with a high DAT/SERT selectivity ratio
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=8AFA9NRL))










> benztropine analog JHW007 could antagonize the reinforcing effects of cocaine by slowly occupying DAT in the CNS
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=WKRVKESA))











> rate of unbinding of compounds at DAT predicts the length of stimulatory effects across different psychostimulants, indicating that binding kinetics can influence behavioral properties of DAT ligands
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=AD7X5MFS))










> improvement in the equilibrium affinity from μM to sub-μM of different modafinil analogs is associated with their slower koff at DAT
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=U37XD9J2))










> replacing the terminal amide of R-modafinil with a thiazole, in combination with substitutions on the bis-phenyl ring system, plays a role in binding kinetics
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=89HXM5S7))





Amide: -CONH2
Thiazole: -C3H3NS
Bisphenyl: 2 benzene rings





> pyrovalerone-based stimulants
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=MQR6DMXK))





Inhibit multiple states of DAT. Similar to ibogaine.






> we cannot conclude if the tested inhibitors might be used for state-dependent modulation of DAT as reported for GBR12909 and benztropine
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=Q9UGBVWU))





benztropine stabilizes Ti state.





> in contrast to R-modafinil, it is the opposite enantiomer that exhibits higher DAT affinity. Given the critical role of sulfoxide configuration in DAT binding for R-modafinil and related compounds, further atomistic studies will be necessary for elucidating the binding interactions of these analogs
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=DXFKN7C8))










> slow dissociation rate could be achieved via the establishment of occluded intermediates, as reported already for LSD in the case of 5HT2b and 5HT2a receptors
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=EKFCPWSU))











> by stabilizing the inward-open state as established in the case of GBR12909 and benztropines
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=YKAXYJAS))










> The molecular consequences of a long residence time at DAT are still enigmatic. Further studies will be needed to clarify this point
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=GQB4EUCW))










> our study did not assess the free-drug concentration for S-MK-26 and (S,S)-CE-158, as was done for R-modafinil and S-CE-123
([10](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/82ZDUWI7?page=10&annotation=KWSIIC8S))





total drug = free drug + tissue drug.

Only free drug is readily available to interact with DAT.
