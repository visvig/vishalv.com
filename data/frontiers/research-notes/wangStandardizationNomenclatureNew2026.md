<span style="color:

Standardized nomenclature for new biomedical technologies is a fundamental prerequisite for implementing the Regulation</span>
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EQLMIM8D?page=1&annotation=6SI2FAXQ))










<span style="color:
#FFF176;">
International Society for Stem Cell Research (ISSCR) has emphasized in its standards that ambiguous naming practices impede the accurate linkage of cell lines to their phenotypic data</span>
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EQLMIM8D?page=1&annotation=ZR43W7BS))










<span style="color:
#FFF176;">
International Society for Cell &amp; Gene Therapy (ISCT) recommended in 2019 that the term be revised to“mesenchymal stromal cells”to more accurately reflect their primary therapeutic mechanism via paracrine and immunomodulatory actions</span>
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EQLMIM8D?page=1&annotation=PESMVN8C))










<span style="color:
#FFF176;">
Japanese Society for Regenerative Medicine (JSRM) has endorsed this terminology, reflecting an international consensus on mechanistically precise nomenclature</span>
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EQLMIM8D?page=1&annotation=CM6422CT))










<span style="color:
#FFF176;">
China currently lacks unified nomenclature standards for new biomedical technologies</span>
([1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EQLMIM8D?page=1&annotation=QK5R3JVL))
